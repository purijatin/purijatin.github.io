Comprehensive Market Ecosystem

From day-ahead planning to real-time balancing, our platform covers the full spectrum of India's power markets under CERC regulations.

⚡

Day-Ahead Market (DAM)

// AUCTION · 10:00–12:00 IST

Bid for next-day electricity delivery through our double-sided closed auction with uniform price discovery across all regions.

  • 96 time-block bidding (15-min slots)
  • Market Coupling for uniform MCP
  • Portfolio & block-level bidding
  • AI-powered price forecasting
🔄

Real-Time Market (RTM)

// 48 SESSIONS · EVERY 30 MIN

Balance supply-demand mismatches in real-time with half-hourly auctions. Delivery within 1 hour of gate closure.

  • 48 intraday auction windows
  • Grid-frequency responsive pricing
  • Automated deviation settlement
  • DISCOM & open-access ready
🌱

Green Markets

// G-DAM · G-TAM · SOLAR · NON-SOLAR

Trade renewable energy with source-specific contracts. Fulfill RPO mandates through dedicated green energy corridors.

  • Solar, Non-solar & Hydro segments
  • Green Term-Ahead contracts
  • RPO compliance tracking
  • Carbon footprint analytics
📜

Certificates (REC & ESCert)

// MONTHLY AUCTION · CERC REGULATED

Trade Renewable Energy Certificates and Energy Saving Certificates to meet regulatory obligations under RPO and PAT schemes.

  • Monthly REC trading sessions
  • Multiplier-based ESCert trading
  • Automated compliance reporting
  • Obligation tracking dashboard
📊

Term-Ahead Market (TAM)

// INTRADAY · DAILY · WEEKLY

Plan your procurement strategy with contracts spanning intraday to weekly delivery periods through continuous trading.

  • Contingency & day-ahead contracts
  • Weekly baseload trading
  • Bilateral matching engine
  • Advanced scheduling tools
🌏

Cross-Border Trading

// NEPAL · BHUTAN · BANGLADESH

Participate in South Asian power trade with seamless cross-border electricity exchange under government frameworks.

  • CBET enabled for SAARC nations
  • Real-time tie-line monitoring
  • Multi-currency settlement
  • Cross-border scheduling API
